Our Story

Ancient grains, carried forward by people who care

SilkGrain began with a simple frustration: the rice and grains that built Central Asian cuisine were nearly impossible to find, fresh and unadulterated, in America.

From a Tashkent kitchen to your table

Our founder grew up watching her grandmother sort devzira rice by hand for the family plov. When she moved to Houston, no store could match what she remembered. So she went back to the source — the farms of the Fergana Valley — and started importing the real thing.

Today SilkGrain works directly with growers across Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan and Kazakhstan, paying fair prices and shipping every harvest fresh from our Houston warehouse.
