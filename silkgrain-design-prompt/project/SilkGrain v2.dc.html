Our story

Rooted in the Silk Road, grown for the modern table

It started with a frustration: the rice and grains that built Central Asian cooking were nearly impossible to find — fresh and unadulterated — in America. So we went back to the source.

Today we partner with growers across Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan and Kazakhstan, paying fairly and shipping every harvest fresh from Houston.
